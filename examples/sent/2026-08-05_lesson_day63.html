Day 63: ReAct Pattern in AI Engineering
TL;DR
The ReAct pattern is a design approach that involves interleaving reasoning and acting in AI systems to improve decision-making and adaptability.

In plain English

The ReAct pattern is based on the idea that an AI system should not just react to its environment, but also reason about its actions and their consequences.
This approach involves alternating between reasoning, which involves planning and decision-making, and acting, which involves executing the planned actions.
By interleaving reasoning and acting, an AI system can adapt to changing circumstances and improve its performance over time.

Think of it this way

The ReAct pattern is similar to a musician improvising a solo, where they alternate between thinking about the next note to play and actually playing it, allowing them to adapt to the rhythm and mood of the music in real-time.

Why it matters for AI Eng interviews

The ReAct pattern is important in AI Engineering because it allows for the development of more flexible and adaptive AI systems, which is a key requirement for many AI Engineering job descriptions, including those that mention classic agent design, such as reasoning, acting, and planning, and is particularly relevant to production concerns such as fine-tuning and observability.
One thing to remember
The key to the ReAct pattern is to interleave reasoning and acting, rather than doing all the reasoning upfront and then acting, which allows for more adaptability and flexibility in AI systems.
