Question 4 · Day 67 · Multi-Agent Systems: Supervisor Pattern
In a multi-agent system, what is the role of the supervisor in the supervisor pattern?
A. To act as a peer among other agents
B. To oversee and coordinate the actions of other agents
C. To compete with other agents for resources
D. To ignore the actions of other agents

Answers

Q1: To measure how well the generated answer aligns with the source information
Faithfulness evaluation in RAG is crucial to ensure that the generated answers accurately reflect the information present in the source, making this the primary purpose of faithfulness evaluation.
Q2: Tool correctness
Tool correctness is indeed a key evaluation metric for agents, as it assesses the agent's ability to correctly apply the tools or methods at its disposal to achieve the desired outcome.
Q3: To simplify complex tasks into manageable sub-tasks
Task decomposition is a fundamental aspect of planning that involves breaking down complex tasks into simpler, more manageable sub-tasks, which can then be addressed individually or in smaller groups.
Q4: To oversee and coordinate the actions of other agents
In the supervisor pattern of multi-agent systems, the supervisor agent plays a crucial role in overseeing and coordinating the actions of other agents, ensuring that the system operates in a cohesive and effective manner.
