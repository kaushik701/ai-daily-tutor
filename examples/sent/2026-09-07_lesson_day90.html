Day 90: Putting It All Together – End‑to‑End AI System Design Checklist
TL;DR
Learn a concise, interview‑ready checklist that moves from requirements through evaluation to production deployment of an AI system.

In plain English

Start by translating business needs into concrete system requirements: data provenance, latency targets, security constraints, and success metrics. Next, design the pipeline – data ingestion, preprocessing, model selection or fine‑tuning, and any Retrieval‑Augmented Generation (RAG) or autonomous agent components. Finally, plan observability, monitoring, and continuous evaluation (evals) so the model can be updated safely in production, respecting MCP (Model‑Control‑Plane) and deployment constraints.
During a system‑design interview, interviewers expect you to articulate each stage, justify trade‑offs, and show how you would verify the system works before launch. A short checklist helps you stay organized and signals that you can bridge research ideas to robust, maintainable services.
The checklist also reminds you to embed evaluation hooks (A/B tests, drift detection) and to plan for fine‑tuning pipelines that can be triggered automatically when performance degrades. Observability tools (metrics, logs, traces) should be defined early so you can meet production SLAs and quickly diagnose issues.

Think of it this way

Designing an AI system is like planning a multi‑course dinner: you first decide the menu (requirements), then gather ingredients and prep stations (data and model pipelines), taste each dish as you cook (continuous evals), and finally serve the meal with a wait staff that monitors guest satisfaction and can adjust seasoning on the fly (observability and fine‑tuning).

In code

def check_system(config):
    required = ['data_source','model','eval_metric','deployment']
    missing = [k for k in required if k not in config]
    return missing

# Example usage
config = {'data_source':'s3','model':'gpt','eval_metric':'accuracy'}
print(check_system(config))
The function flags any missing checklist items, letting you catch gaps before building or deploying the system.

Why it matters for AI Eng interviews

Interviewers look for concrete signals that you understand RAG pipelines, autonomous agents, rigorous evals, fine‑tuning loops, observability stacks, and MCP‑style production controls; a clear end‑to‑end checklist demonstrates that you can translate high‑level requirements into a production‑ready AI service.
One thing to remember
A well‑structured checklist bridges business goals to reliable, observable AI deployments.
