Day 56: Optimizing Inference with KV Caching for Faster Generation
TL;DR
KV caching is a technique used to speed up the generation process in large language models by storing and reusing previously computed key-value pairs.

In plain English

KV caching is a method that helps large language models generate text faster by avoiding redundant computations.
This is particularly useful in models that use attention mechanisms, where the same keys and values are computed multiple times.
By storing these pre-computed values, the model can quickly retrieve them instead of recalculating, resulting in significant speed improvements.

Think of it this way

Think of KV caching like a chef who prepares a dish with multiple identical sauces. Instead of making each sauce from scratch every time, the chef can store a batch of the sauce in the fridge and reuse it when needed, saving time and effort.

Why it matters for AI Eng interviews

KV caching is crucial in AI engineering and ML engineering roles, especially when dealing with large language models, as it directly impacts the model's performance and efficiency. Job descriptions often mention requirements like inference optimization, RAG, agents, and fine-tuning, all of which can benefit from KV caching, making it an essential concept to understand for production concerns and model deployment.
One thing to remember
KV caching accelerates generation by reusing pre-computed key-value pairs, making it a vital technique for optimizing inference in large language models.
