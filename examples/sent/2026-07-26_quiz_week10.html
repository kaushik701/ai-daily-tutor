Question 3 · Day 52 · RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)

Question 4 · Day 50 · Parameter-Efficient Fine-tuning (LoRA, QLoRA)
What is a key advantage of using LoRA or QLoRA for fine-tuning large language models?
A. They require full model retraining for each new task
B. They are computationally expensive and require significant resources
C. They enable efficient adaptation of pre-trained models to new tasks with minimal parameters updated
D. They are limited to specific types of language models

Answers

Q1: To ensure AI systems align with human values and principles
Constitutional AI aims to develop AI systems that are aligned with human values and principles, ensuring that they operate within a framework that respects and promotes human well-being.
Q2: The policy directly to maximize cumulative rewards
DPO directly optimizes the policy to maximize cumulative rewards, bypassing the need for a value function or explicit reward modeling, which can be particularly useful in situations where specifying a reward function is challenging.
Q3: Through human feedback on model outputs
RLHF improves model performance by incorporating human feedback on the model's outputs, allowing the model to learn from direct human evaluation and adjust its performance accordingly.
Q4: They enable efficient adaptation of pre-trained models to new tasks with minimal parameters updated
LoRA and QLoRA are parameter-efficient fine-tuning methods that allow for the adaptation of large pre-trained models to new tasks by updating only a small subset of parameters, making them computationally efficient and resource-friendly.
