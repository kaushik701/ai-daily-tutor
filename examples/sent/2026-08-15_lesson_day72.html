Day 72: Evaluating RAG Models with Faithfulness, Context Relevance, and Answer Relevance
TL;DR
Evaluating RAG models involves assessing their faithfulness, context relevance, and answer relevance to ensure they provide accurate and relevant information.

In plain English

The RAGAS triad, which includes Faithfulness, Context Relevance, and Answer Relevance, is crucial in evaluating the performance of RAG models. Faithfulness measures how well the generated answers align with the knowledge source. Context Relevance evaluates how relevant the generated answers are to the given context. Answer Relevance, on the other hand, assesses how well the generated answers address the user's question.
These metrics are important because they help identify potential issues with the RAG model, such as providing inaccurate or irrelevant information. By evaluating these metrics, developers can refine their models to improve their overall performance and reliability.
In the context of RAG models, evaluating these metrics is an ongoing process that requires continuous monitoring and refinement to ensure the models provide high-quality and relevant information.

Think of it this way

Evaluating RAG models is like being a restaurant critic, where you assess the quality of the food (answers) based on its taste (faithfulness), presentation (context relevance), and how well it satisfies your hunger (answer relevance). Just as a good restaurant critic considers these factors to provide a comprehensive review, evaluating RAG models involves assessing these metrics to ensure they provide accurate and relevant information.

Why it matters for AI Eng interviews

Evaluating RAG models with faithfulness, context relevance, and answer relevance is crucial in AI engineering roles, as it directly impacts the quality of the model's output. Job descriptions often highlight the importance of model evaluation, fine-tuning, and observability, and being able to discuss these metrics and how they are used to improve model performance is essential for success in these roles.
One thing to remember
Faithfulness, context relevance, and answer relevance are interconnected metrics that must be evaluated together to get a comprehensive understanding of a RAG model's performance.
