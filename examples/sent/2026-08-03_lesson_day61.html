Day 61: Introduction to AI Agents in Engineering
TL;DR
An AI agent is a program that uses tools and data to make decisions and take actions autonomously, often in a loop of observation, evaluation, and improvement.

In plain English

In AI engineering, an agent refers to a system that perceives its environment and takes actions to achieve specific goals. This can range from simple autonomous vehicles to complex chatbots that learn from user interactions. The key characteristic of an agent is its ability to operate with a degree of autonomy, using tools and data to make decisions and improve its performance over time.
Agents can be categorized based on their capabilities and the level of autonomy they exhibit. For instance, a simple reflex agent reacts to the current state of the environment without considering future consequences, while a more complex agent might use machine learning models to predict outcomes and adjust its actions accordingly.
The integration of Large Language Models (LLMs) with other tools and the ability to engage in a loop of observation, evaluation, and improvement is a critical aspect of modern AI agents, enabling them to learn from their interactions and adapt to new situations.

Think of it this way

The operation of an AI agent can be likened to a chef in a kitchen. The chef (agent) observes the current state of the kitchen (environment), decides what dishes to prepare (actions), and uses various tools and ingredients (data and algorithms) to achieve the goal of serving a satisfying meal. Just as a skilled chef continually evaluates the dishes and adjusts the recipes based on feedback, an AI agent learns from its interactions and fine-tunes its decisions to better achieve its objectives.

Why it matters for AI Eng interviews

Understanding AI agents is crucial for AI Engineering roles as it directly relates to the development and deployment of autonomous systems, such as those involving RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) and other advanced technologies. The ability to design, evaluate, and fine-tune AI agents is a key requirement in job descriptions for AI Engineers, who must consider aspects like observability, model performance, and production concerns to ensure that these systems operate effectively and efficiently.
One thing to remember
The core of an AI agent's functionality is its ability to perceive, decide, and act autonomously, making it a fundamental concept in AI engineering with applications across various domains.
