Day 71: Evaluating Agents Beyond Accuracy
TL;DR
Evaluating agents involves assessing not only their accuracy but also their ability to use the right tools and complete tasks efficiently.

In plain English

When we evaluate agents, such as those in robotics or software, we need to consider more than just their accuracy. We also need to assess whether they are using the correct tools for the task at hand and whether they can complete tasks efficiently. This is important because an agent that is highly accurate but uses the wrong tools or takes too long to complete a task may not be effective in practice.
In addition to accuracy, we need to evaluate agents based on their ability to select and use the appropriate tools, as well as their ability to complete tasks within a reasonable timeframe. This requires a more nuanced evaluation framework that goes beyond simple accuracy metrics.
This framework should include metrics such as tool correctness, task completion rate, and efficiency, which can provide a more comprehensive understanding of an agent's performance.

Think of it this way

Think of it like a chef in a kitchen. While it's great if the chef can chop vegetables quickly and accurately, it's also important that they use the right knife for the job and can complete the entire dish within a reasonable timeframe. If they use the wrong knife or take too long, the dish may not turn out well, even if the individual components are prepared accurately.

Why it matters for AI Eng interviews

Evaluating agents beyond accuracy is critical in AI Engineering roles, where the ability to design and deploy effective agents can have a significant impact on business outcomes. Job descriptions often require experience with evals, RAG (Red, Amber, Green) status, and agents, as well as fine-tuning and production concerns, highlighting the importance of considering tool correctness and task completion in agent evaluation.
One thing to remember
When evaluating agents, it's essential to consider not only their accuracy but also their ability to use the right tools and complete tasks efficiently.
